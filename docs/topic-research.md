# Topic Research

A topic only qualifies if a video about it already proved the audience cares.

## The criteria

- The topic has a video with **1–2 million views**.
- Those views came in the **past 6 months** (not the past year).
- The video is **still gaining views now** — check the recent growth, not just the total.
- vidIQ (free plan) can be used to check the growth record.

## Take your time

Don't rush the research. A rushed pick costs a whole day.

## Small channels are a strong signal

Also analyse small channels with low subscriber counts that are getting far more views than their size. A topic that broke out on a small channel is a strong signal — the story itself is doing the work, not the channel's audience.

## What a good pick looks like

- A clear hook in one sentence ("Why didn't Napoleon just stop?")
- A story with real stakes and a surprising turn, not just a list of facts
- Dates and numbers that can be verified against real sources
- No current-news topics, no unverified claims

## Examples from the channel

- Napoleon — "Why didn't Napoleon just stop?" (History Matters, ~1.59M views)
- The Korean War — "The War America Forgot" (~1.18M views, still growing fast)
- The Eagle's Nest fire, October 2026 — a current event with a historical building at its centre

## What to avoid

- Topics whose only big video is over 6 months old
- Topics under 1M views, unless a small-channel breakout is clearly accelerating toward it
- Topics over 2M views — the window is 1–2M
- Re-presenting a topic that was already picked or already rejected
