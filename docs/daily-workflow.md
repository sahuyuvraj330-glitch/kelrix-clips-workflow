# Daily Workflow

One Short per day, same loop every day.

## The cycle

1. **9:00 AM IST — topic research.** Research YouTube for past events and historical stories. See [topic-research.md](topic-research.md).
2. **Message the picks** to the channel owner so they can choose.
3. **Owner picks** a topic and sends it back.
4. **Start the Claude workflow** (fact-check, then script + storyboard) and **make the voiceover**, then send the voiceover to the owner.
   - **Voiceover approval rule:** if the owner does not reply within 1 hour, the voiceover is auto-approved and the work proceeds.
5. **If Claude's free usage runs out**, wait for the resume time and set an automatic resume for it.
6. **When it resumes, continue the workflow and send the QC sheet** to the owner for approval. The QC sheet has **no auto-approve** — the owner approves it themselves.
7. **Render the video.**
8. **Add sound effects and background music automatically** when the video is ready — no approval needed for this step.
9. **Go to YouTube Studio** when the video is fully ready.
10. **Upload** with title, description, hashtags and thumbnail.
11. **Set the publish timer** in YouTube Studio so it auto-publishes at the best US time.
12. **Repeat the next day.**
13. **Captions are part of the render** — simple, rounded, white, burned in. They are specified in the animation brief, not added afterwards.

## Approval gates

| Step | Approval |
|---|---|
| Script + storyboard | Owner approves |
| Voiceover | Owner approves, or auto-approves after 1 hour with no reply |
| QC sheet | Owner approves — never auto-approves |
| Music + sound effects | Automatic, no approval |
| Upload | Automatic once the video and thumbnail are ready |

## Standing rules

- Animation is done in Claude in the browser only, step by step.
- Voiceover: Helio, same voice and pace for every Short. See [voiceover.md](voiceover.md).
- Thumbnails: fresh standalone designs in the video's own cutout style. See [thumbnails.md](thumbnails.md).
- Upload route: YouTube Studio in the browser (free). See [upload-and-scheduling.md](upload-and-scheduling.md).
