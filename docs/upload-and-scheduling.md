# Upload and Scheduling

Uploads go through YouTube Studio in the browser — free, no third-party uploader.

## Upload checklist

1. Upload the finished video file (verified with ffprobe first).
2. Set the **title** exactly as approved.
3. Paste the **description** exactly as approved, including the sources line.
4. Add the **hashtags** exactly as approved.
5. Set the **custom thumbnail** (the approved one, 1152×2048).
6. Audience: **"No, it's not made for kids."**
7. Check for copyright issues before scheduling.
8. **Set the publish timer** — never publish immediately by accident.
9. After scheduling, **reload the video page fully** and confirm the thumbnail is still applied.

## Best times (US audience)

Researched from Buffer, SocialPilot, FeedHive, vidIQ and others:

- **Strongest: Friday 4–7 PM US local time.**
- Weekday evenings **6–9 PM US** is the safe default.
- Midday **12–2 PM US** is also strong.
- Avoid 3–7 AM US, and Sunday evenings.
- Once the channel has data, the real best time comes from YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube".

IST equivalents (US on EST, 10.5 hours behind IST):

- Friday 4–7 PM EST = Saturday 2:30–5:30 AM IST
- Weekday 6–9 PM EST = 4:30–7:30 AM IST next day
- 12–2 PM EST = 10:30 PM–12:30 AM IST

Our Shorts go out at **11:00 PM IST**, which catches the US midday scroll.

## Cross-posting

The same Shorts also go to Instagram Reels, Threads and Facebook as **Kelrix Clips** (`@kelrixclips`):

- Instagram Reels: one a day at **8:00 PM IST**.
- Reels captions point to the YouTube channel (`YouTube: @Kelrix`) because the Instagram bio can't carry a clickable YouTube link from the web — that link can only be added in the Instagram mobile app.
- Threads posts keep the YouTube link, since Threads links work.
- Post a Short to the other platforms only after its YouTube upload is done.
