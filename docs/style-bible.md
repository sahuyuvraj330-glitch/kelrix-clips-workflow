# Kelrix Style Bible

The visual specification for every Kelrix Short. If a frame doesn't match this document, it's wrong — fix the frame, not the document.

## Format

- **1080×1920, 30fps, 9:16.** Vertical only.
- **~60–82 seconds**, 7 scenes.
- Scene cuts use a short fade through black; the narration keeps talking across the cut.

## Characters — the cutout figure

Every person in a Kelrix video is built the same way:

- **Tall, slim figure** with a **small round head**. Never a big head, never chibi proportions.
- **Dot eyes**, a **single-line mouth**. No detailed facial features, no realistic faces.
- **Jointed limbs with visible circle joints** at the shoulders, elbows, hips and knees.
- **Flat solid colours**, one fill per body part. **Thin dark outlines** throughout.
- Costume and props match the period and the person — a captain's coat, a POW shirt, a bicorne that sits on the head, not floating above it.
- Characters are cutouts, not likenesses. They read as the person through costume, setting and labels — never through a realistic face.

## Colour and backgrounds

- Flat solid fills, a limited palette per scene. One dominant background colour per scene; the figure must read clearly against it.
- Backgrounds are simple flat shapes — stylised buildings, landscapes and interiors, never photorealistic.
- **Maps are stylised outlines**, not real maps — a simplified country shape with the neighbours named and the key places marked.
- Banners and flags are **solid colours with a simple emblem**, never real national flags.

## On-screen text

- **Cream label boxes** with dark text for facts, names and scene titles.
- **Counters** for the numbers that matter (600,000 → 100,000; 36,574).
- **Stamps** for the turning points: OFFER WITHDRAWN, REFUSED, NO PEACE TREATY.
- **Captions: white bold text on a soft dark rounded pill**, burned into the video. Short phrases, timed to the narration.

## What never appears

- Clay texture, paper texture, or any textured look.
- Detailed realistic cartoon faces.
- Big-head chibi proportions.
- Swastikas or Nazi insignia — all insignia generic.
- Anything that glorifies a regime, a building, or a war. The story is told straight; the facts carry it.

## Audio

- Narration sits clearly above the music and effects bed.
- Target loudness **~−14 LUFS**, true peak under **−1 dBTP**.
- Original music and scene sound effects, no library tracks with lyrics.

## The test for any frame

1. Could a viewer who has never seen the channel tell this is a Kelrix frame? (Cutout figure, flat colours, cream labels.)
2. Does the frame tell part of the story without the narration?
3. Is the subject doing something, or just standing there?
